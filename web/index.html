What this evidence means

Sources: local Ookla performance extract, geoBoundaries LGA boundaries, GRID3 health facilities and HOT OSM education records.

Screening, not prediction

The score combines upload deficit (70%, against 2.5 Mbps) and latency deficit (30%, from 50 to 250 ms). Scores of 35 and 65 define screening categories. These are illustrative thresholds, not validated BVAS requirements.

Unknown is a separate state

Areas without usable observations remain unscored. They receive verification priority 75 to encourage field investigation. A larger sample means at least 10 tiles and 30 tests; it does not establish freshness.

Use the right interpretation

Observation dates and mobile/fixed provenance are unverified. Schools are not verified polling units. Distances are straight-line proximity to historical observations, not road routes or verified upload facilities.

Your workspace

Bookmarks and a saved comparison stay in this browser. The analytical service uses a read-only evidence snapshot. No live election results or voter records are collected.
